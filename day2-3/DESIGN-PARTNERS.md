## Explicit non-targets (anti-ICP reminder)

- Pure merchants on Stripe Checkout with no in-house payment controls.
- Generic GRC tool buyers wanting “ISO + SOC 2 + everything.”
- Anyone asking us to vault CHD or replace their QSA.
